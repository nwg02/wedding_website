FAQs

What is the dress code?

Formal attire is requested of all guests.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by April 1, 2027.

Can I bring a guest?

Please refer to your invitation for +1 details.

Are kids invited?

Yes! All children are invited. Chick-fil-A will be provided for them.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive around 30 minutes before the ceremony.

Where do I park?

Holy Spirit Catholic Church has a parking lot and plenty of parking about the building. Emory Conference Center has a 2 level parking garage (go down the hill immediately to the left of the main entrance of the hotel).

Will there be transportation between the church and venue?

To be determined. There may be future plans to provide a shuttle to and from the church and venue.

Indoor or outdoor wedding?

The ceremony and reception will be indoors. In case you love outdoors, the reception has a nice covered patio as well.

Can I take photos at the church?

Do not take photos during the wedding ceremony. We have an amazing photographer.

How do I get to the reception from the church?

The venue, on Emory University's campus, is about a 30 minutes drive from the church. We have a considerable time gap between the ceremony and reception which should give you enough time to arrive and plan before.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Please note on your RSVP your dietary restriction.

How is dinner served and is there an open bar?

Dinner will be plated and will be selected by each guest on their RSVP. There is an open bar for 4 hours.

Can I wear white?

No.
